# 📋 Dear Data

> This project is a **work in progress**. I have not finished my data collection or sketch yet.

## 🌟 About

### What I am collecting data about

- I am recording the **peak MMR** of my **Rocket League** competitive solo queue 2v2 teammates. As the **competitive season** progresses, I run into teammates with varying MMRs, as higher ranked players gain more MMR and the leaderboards become more spread out.
- In addition to recording my teammates' peak MMR, I am also recording **interesting observations** related to the match/my teammate, including: *Points difference, Win or Loss, Cool tags etc*.

### Why this topic interests me

- I decided to focus my data collection around Rocket League because I have put many hours into this game and I am **passionate** to improve at it. Playing Rocket League is a hobby that I enjoy, and I was interested to study the **ranks** of my teammates that I come across. 
- This data collection process allowed me to notice that I get the opportunity to play with much **higher ranked** players, and sometimes I have to play with much lower ranked players. Getting a higher ranked player on my team does not always equate to a win; the opposing team will usually have one higher ranked player, and my own skill influences this too.
- The biggest complaint of many Rocket League players is that their teammates are **holding them back** from ranking up. Through my data collection, I wanted to make it visible that your teammates are **not** always the issue.

### My data visualisation

- I am still finishing my data collection, so I have not finalised my idea for how I will visualise my data.
- However, my current plan is to show a **graph** of MMR against days into the competitive season, where the viewer is able to **interact** by hovering over data points to bring up an **on-screen interface** showing attributes of each data (Points differece, Win or Loss etc.)

## Context

Of course, I expect that **Rocket League** is a completely **new** topic to you and many ideas I bring up do not make sense to you.

- I plan to create a small, concise word **document** providing context on the **ranked system** and how it works in Rocket League. I will link this in my README and in my repository.
